Amazon Sales Performance Dashboard
Executive Summary
This project features a commercial-grade Power BI dashboard analyzing $12.64M in global Amazon sales data to evaluate revenue trends, product profitability, and regional delivery performance. Refactored from legacy visuals, the report uses custom DAX measures and an optimized layout to deliver clear, high-impact business insights without canvas clutter.

Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
Total Revenue: $12.64M

Total Profit: $1.47M

Profit Margin: 11.61%

Average Order Value (AOV): $504.99

Technical Features & Data Modeling
Explicit DAX Architecture: Built dedicated DAX measures for dynamic, accurate calculations of sales, net profit, profit margins, and transaction averages.

Optimized Visual Layout: Replaced high-density pie and doughnut charts with structured horizontal bar charts and market treemaps to improve comparative analysis.

Interactive Filtering: Configured compact dropdown slicers for Category and Market parameters to maintain a clean visual hierarchy.

Key Business Insights & Recommendations
Top Revenue Drivers: APAC leads global sales volume at $3.59M, followed by the EU market at $2.94M. Technology remains the top-performing product category.

Profitability Focus: The global profit margin stands at 11.61%, with high fulfillment overhead suppressing net returns in secondary markets.

Logistics Optimization: Standard Class handles the bulk of shipping volume. Operational re-evaluation of Same Day delivery pricing in low-volume regions like Canada and Africa is recommended to protect margins.
